Interactive lessons for drawing motor control circuits. Every chapter has simulations that show what a drawing program cannot, such as the rotating field, the starting current and the voltage a winding gets; worked problems that open one step at a time; and lab sheets for drawing and simulating the circuits in CADe SIMU with IEC symbols.
