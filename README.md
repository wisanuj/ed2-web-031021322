# ED2_Web — Electrical Drawing II (031021322) teaching site

Bilingual (Thai / English) interactive pages in the style of IE_Web. Live site: https://wisanuj.github.io/ed2-web-031021322/ · local preview: launch config `ed2-web` (port 8795).

## Files
- `index.html` roadmap; chapter 1 pages `ch1_1_three_phase.html` … `ch1_4_cadesimu.html`; chapter 2 pages `ch2_1_symbols.html`, `ch2_2_terminal_numbers.html`, `ch2_3_drawings.html`, `ch2_4_control_types.html`, `ch2_5_cadesimu.html`; chapter 3 pages `ch3_1_push_buttons.html`, `ch3_2_selectors.html`, `ch3_3_multi_station.html`, `ch3_4_cadesimu.html`; chapter 4 pages `ch4_1_contactors.html`, `ch4_2_relays.html`, `ch4_3_timers.html`, `ch4_4_overload.html`, `ch4_5_cadesimu.html`; chapter 5 pages `ch5_1_self_holding.html`, `ch5_2_protection_lamps.html`, `ch5_3_jogging.html`, `ch5_4_single_phase.html`, `ch5_5_fault_finding.html` (12 miswired DOL circuits with symptom, cause, fix and a test run on both circuits), `ch5_6_wiring.html`, `ch5_7_cadesimu.html` (lab page: CADe SIMU how-to, name map, the control-supply and default-name mistakes run in RL.Sim).
- `assets/shell.js`, `lesson.js`, `draw.js`, `ee.css`: copied from IE_Web (visited/counter keys renamed `ed2-*`).
- `assets/ed.js`: numbers, canvas helpers, numeric and multiple-choice quizzes, per-unit induction-motor model (rated slip 3.3 %, starting current 6.5×, starting torque 2.2×, breakdown 3.4×).
- `assets/ed1.js`: chapter 1 widgets: `Field` (rotating field, any poles), `TorqueSpeed`, `plate` (nameplates), `TBox` (terminal box solved by nodal analysis), `Field2` (single-phase field), `Start1` (single-phase start-up with centrifugal switch and faults).
- `assets/iec.js`: IEC 60617 symbols in vertical current paths, drawn in their real state (contacts, operators, timed contacts, coils, lamps, fuses, breakers, thermal elements, motor; from chapter 5 also windings, capacitor, earth and earth fault, meter, current transformer and the centrifugal operator).
- `assets/relay.js`: relay-logic simulator `RL.Sim` (chapter 2 on): grid circuits between L and N, push buttons/selectors/sensors/overload, coils and timers, short-circuit detection (control fuse blows), live and current colouring, current-path numbers, automatic contact cross-reference under coils, wire (potential) numbers, print and fill-in options (`print`, `noNames`, `noTerms`, `noPaths`, `noXref`, `wireNos`). Chapter 5 additions: `o.hiWire` (one wire number highlighted), `o.railNos`, `o.noPower`, `sim.nodesOf(n)`, `sim.wireLabel(n)` (N shown as "N"), `sim.termsOf(n)`, `sim.setMid(id, bool)` and the device option `midT` (a push button between its positions), `sim.setMains(v)` (power cut ahead of Q1), the `fault` device with the `earth` element (earth fault to N), the full three-phase power drawing (`power.full`: L1 L2 L3 N PE, terminal numbers, control tap after or before Q1) and the single-phase drawing (`power.phases: 1`: two-pole Q1/KM1, F2 poles in series, run and start windings, centrifugal switch, capacitor).
- `assets/ch2.js`: symbol gallery with the codes of every system, device terminal labels, the DOL, two-wire, three-wire and float-switch circuits.
- `assets/ch3.js`: chapter 3 widgets: `C3.PB` (cut-away push button, break-before-make, E-stop latch), `C3.Knob` (maintained or spring-return selector), `C3.chart` (contact chart), `C3.Drum` (drum-switch reversing, EMC9 Fig. 5-8), circuits `SELLAMPS`, `PBLAMPS`, `STATION` (correct / starts in series / stops in parallel) with `stationTrials`, `HOA`.
- `assets/ch4.js`: chapter 4 widgets: `C4.Contactor` (contactor cut open: coil voltage, pick-up/drop-out, inrush current graph, contact timing), `C4.Timing` / `C4.timingSVG` (live and static timing diagrams of on-delay and off-delay timers), `C4.Overload` + `C4.thermalSVG` + `C4.tripCurve` (first-order bimetal heat model, trip classes 10A/10/20/30 tuned to the IEC 60947-4-1 limits, TEST/RESET/auto reset), circuits `COILTEST`, `RELAY`, `ONDELAY`, `OFFDELAY`, `INSTANT` (timed vs instantaneous timer contact). In relay.js a plain (non-`td`) contact of a timer is instantaneous.
- `assets/ch5.js`: chapter 5 (namespace `C5`): circuits `SHEET(n)` (the lab-file steps in course names), `DOL5`, `PHASE`, `J1`–`J3` (jogging), `ONEPH`, `STATIONS`, `SHEETINFO`, `NAMEMAP`; `FAULTS` (10 fault-finding cases) with `runSeq` / `faultTrial`; `panel` (hold buttons, toggles, power-failure button, status) and `powerCut`; widgets `Start` (DOL start-up against the overload trip), `JogRace` (jog-button contact race) and `Phase` (phase-failure relay F3 with phase lamps).
- `assets/wiring.js` + `assets/wiring.css`: namespace `WD`, wiring diagrams (destination addresses or point-to-point lines) and IEC 61082-1 terminal tables built from any RL.Sim spec plus a layout (`WD.Wiring`, `WD.terminalTable`); used on page 5.6, written to be reused in chapters 6–13.
- `assets/dev.js` + `assets/devdata.js`: real-device photo cards (photo with numbered markers, legend, IEC symbol, terminal numbers, credit). `devdata.js` is generated by `Docs/make_devices.py`; do not edit it by hand. Used on pages 2.1 (all 12 devices), 3.1 (`pb`, `lamp`) and 3.2 (`sel`).
- `assets/sym.js`: IEC 60617-style SVG symbols (fuse, breaker, motor, supply rails, terminal boxes); also used to render the figures of the Word documents.
- `assets/panel.js`: `PN.Door(el, sim, o)` the panel door of a relay simulator drawn as real devices seen from the front (push buttons with coloured caps that sink while held, latching emergency stop, pilot lamps that glow in their colour, selector knobs), in step with the drawing; used on every simulator with buttons or lamps (chapters 2–5; `C5.panel` mounts it automatically).
- `assets/stops.js`: the table of pages and planned chapters; `assets/ed2.css`: site-specific styles.
- `img/`: `cadesimu/` (screenshots), `lab/` (unwired sheets of the instructor's CADe SIMU lab file; `lab1_sheet9.jpg` on page 5.7), `motor/` (department photos of the lab motor), `book/` (textbook figures, cited under each), `dev/` (Wikimedia Commons photos cropped by `Docs/make_devices.py`; author, file page and licence under each photo; CC BY-SA adaptations are shared under the same licence).
- No documents are published on the site: lab sheets are handed out on paper in class.

## Conventions
- Static text in `data-l="th"` / `data-l="en"` blocks; script strings `MC.t(th, en)`. EN mode holds no Thai except the language button.
- Canvas pictures of motors are seen from the drive end (IEC 60034-8: L1→U1, L2→V1, L3→W1 turns clockwise).
- Device names in chapter 1 follow the instructor's CADe SIMU lab files (F, CB, M); the course-wide letter convention is still to be decided (chapter 2).
- Images: textbook and web figures carry a source line; no watermarked images.
- Course device letters from chapter 2: Q F KM KA KT S H M X; the new IEC 81346-2 codes (QA FC KF SF BG PF MA XD) are taught alongside (codes checked against the standard's Table 2, 2009 edition).
- Cache tag `?v=12`; pages of chapter 2 use the head snippet `_head2.txt` (adds iec.js, relay.js, ch2.js); chapter 3 uses `_head3.txt` (adds ch3.js, devdata.js, dev.js), chapter 4 `_head4.txt` (adds ch4.js) and chapter 5 `_head5.txt` (adds ch5.js, wiring.js, wiring.css). Page 2.1 also loads devdata.js and dev.js.
- Chapter 5 draws every circuit with the course names and IEC numbering (control circuit 230 V between L1 and N, fed after Q1; contacts on the L side, A2 and X2 straight to N; lamps green run, yellow overload, white stopped). The names of the instructor's lab files appear only in the name map on page 5.7.
- Push-button colours follow IEC 60204-1:2016 (start white preferred, never red; stop black preferred, never green; red on yellow only for the E-stop).
