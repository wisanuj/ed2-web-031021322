## Conventions
- Static text in `data-l="th"` / `data-l="en"` blocks; script strings `MC.t(th, en)`. EN mode holds no Thai except the language button.
- Canvas pictures of motors are seen from the drive end (IEC 60034-8: L1→U1, L2→V1, L3→W1 turns clockwise).
- Device names in chapter 1 follow the instructor's CADe SIMU lab files (F, CB, M); the course-wide letter convention is still to be decided (chapter 2).
- Images: textbook and web figures carry a source line; no watermarked images.
- Course device letters from chapter 2: Q F KM KA KT S H M X; the new IEC 81346-2 codes (QA FC KF SF BG PF MA XD) are taught alongside (codes checked against the standard's Table 2, 2009 edition).
- Cache tag `?v=17`; pages of chapter 2 use the head snippet `_head2.txt` (adds iec.js, relay.js, ch2.js); chapter 3 uses `_head3.txt` (adds ch3.js, devdata.js, dev.js), chapter 4 `_head4.txt` (adds ch4.js), chapter 5 `_head5.txt` (adds ch5.js, wiring.js, wiring.css), chapter 6 `_head6.txt` (adds ch6.js), chapter 7 `_head7.txt` (adds ch7.js) and the MCP pages `_head_mcp.txt` (adds mcp.css and mcp.js after the chapter 7 set). Page 2.1 also loads devdata.js and dev.js.
- Chapter 5 draws every circuit with the course names and IEC numbering (control circuit 230 V between L1 and N, fed after Q1; contacts on the L side, A2 and X2 straight to N; lamps green run, yellow overload, white stopped). The names of the instructor's lab files appear only in the name map on page 5.7.
- Push-button colours follow IEC 60204-1:2016 (start white preferred, never red; stop black preferred, never green; red on yellow only for the E-stop).
