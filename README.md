# ED2_Web — Electrical Drawing II (031021322) teaching site

Bilingual (Thai / English) interactive pages in the style of IE_Web. Live site: https://wisanuj.github.io/ed2-web-031021322/ · local preview: launch config `ed2-web` (port 8795).

## Files
- `index.html` roadmap; chapter 1 pages `ch1_1_three_phase.html` … `ch1_4_cadesimu.html`; chapter 2 pages `ch2_1_symbols.html`, `ch2_2_terminal_numbers.html`, `ch2_3_drawings.html`, `ch2_4_control_types.html`, `ch2_5_cadesimu.html`; chapter 3 pages `ch3_1_push_buttons.html`, `ch3_2_selectors.html`, `ch3_3_multi_station.html`, `ch3_4_cadesimu.html`; chapter 4 pages `ch4_1_contactors.html`, `ch4_2_relays.html`, `ch4_3_timers.html`, `ch4_4_overload.html`, `ch4_5_cadesimu.html`.
- `assets/shell.js`, `lesson.js`, `draw.js`, `ee.css`: copied from IE_Web (visited/counter keys renamed `ed2-*`).
- `assets/ed.js`: numbers, canvas helpers, numeric and multiple-choice quizzes, per-unit induction-motor model (rated slip 3.3 %, starting current 6.5×, starting torque 2.2×, breakdown 3.4×).
- `assets/ed1.js`: chapter 1 widgets: `Field` (rotating field, any poles), `TorqueSpeed`, `plate` (nameplates), `TBox` (terminal box solved by nodal analysis), `Field2` (single-phase field), `Start1` (single-phase start-up with centrifugal switch and faults).
- `assets/iec.js`: IEC 60617 symbols in vertical current paths, drawn in their real state (contacts, operators, timed contacts, coils, lamps, fuses, breakers, thermal elements, motor).
- `assets/relay.js`: relay-logic simulator `RL.Sim` (chapter 2 on): grid circuits between L and N, push buttons/selectors/sensors/overload, coils and timers, short-circuit detection (control fuse blows), live and current colouring, current-path numbers, automatic contact cross-reference under coils, wire (potential) numbers, print and fill-in options (`print`, `noNames`, `noTerms`, `noPaths`, `noXref`, `wireNos`).
- `assets/ch2.js`: symbol gallery with the codes of every system, device terminal labels, the DOL, two-wire, three-wire and float-switch circuits.
- `assets/ch3.js`: chapter 3 widgets: `C3.PB` (cut-away push button, break-before-make, E-stop latch), `C3.Knob` (maintained or spring-return selector), `C3.chart` (contact chart), `C3.Drum` (drum-switch reversing, EMC9 Fig. 5-8), circuits `SELLAMPS`, `PBLAMPS`, `STATION` (correct / starts in series / stops in parallel) with `stationTrials`, `HOA`.
- `assets/ch4.js`: chapter 4 widgets: `C4.Contactor` (contactor cut open: coil voltage, pick-up/drop-out, inrush current graph, contact timing), `C4.Timing` / `C4.timingSVG` (live and static timing diagrams of on-delay and off-delay timers), `C4.Overload` + `C4.thermalSVG` + `C4.tripCurve` (first-order bimetal heat model, trip classes 10A/10/20/30 tuned to the IEC 60947-4-1 limits, TEST/RESET/auto reset), circuits `COILTEST`, `RELAY`, `ONDELAY`, `OFFDELAY`, `INSTANT` (timed vs instantaneous timer contact). In relay.js a plain (non-`td`) contact of a timer is instantaneous.
- `assets/dev.js` + `assets/devdata.js`: real-device photo cards (photo with numbered markers, legend, IEC symbol, terminal numbers, credit). `devdata.js` is generated by `Docs/make_devices.py`; do not edit it by hand. Used on pages 2.1 (all 12 devices), 3.1 (`pb`, `lamp`) and 3.2 (`sel`).
- `assets/sym.js`: IEC 60617-style SVG symbols (fuse, breaker, motor, supply rails, terminal boxes); also used to render the figures of the Word documents.
- `assets/stops.js`: the table of pages and planned chapters; `assets/ed2.css`: site-specific styles.
- `img/`: `cadesimu/` (screenshots), `lab/` (instructor's CADe SIMU lab sheets), `motor/` (department photos of the lab motor), `book/` (textbook figures, cited under each), `dev/` (Wikimedia Commons photos cropped by `Docs/make_devices.py`; author, file page and licence under each photo; CC BY-SA adaptations are shared under the same licence).
- No documents are published on the site: lab sheets are handed out on paper in class.

## Conventions
- Static text in `data-l="th"` / `data-l="en"` blocks; script strings `MC.t(th, en)`. EN mode holds no Thai except the language button.
- Canvas pictures of motors are seen from the drive end (IEC 60034-8: L1→U1, L2→V1, L3→W1 turns clockwise).
- Device names in chapter 1 follow the instructor's CADe SIMU lab files (F, CB, M); the course-wide letter convention is still to be decided (chapter 2).
- Images: textbook and web figures carry a source line; no watermarked images.
- Course device letters from chapter 2: Q F KM KA KT S H M X; the new IEC 81346-2 codes (QA FC KF SF BG PF MA XD) are taught alongside (codes checked against the standard's Table 2, 2009 edition).
- Cache tag `?v=9`; pages of chapter 2 use the head snippet `_head2.txt` (adds iec.js, relay.js, ch2.js); chapter 3 uses `_head3.txt` (adds ch3.js, devdata.js, dev.js) and chapter 4 `_head4.txt` (adds ch4.js). Page 2.1 also loads devdata.js and dev.js.
- Push-button colours follow IEC 60204-1:2016 (start white preferred, never red; stop black preferred, never green; red on yellow only for the E-stop).
